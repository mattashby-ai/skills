## Delivery Model Guidance

| Model | When to Use | Margin Protection |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| Time & Materials (T&M) | Unclear or evolving scope | Bill actuals; invoice fortnightly |
| Fixed Price | Well-defined, bounded scope | Add 15–20% contingency; document all assumptions |
| Milestone-Based | Multi-phase delivery with clear gates | Define objective acceptance criteria per milestone |
| Retainer | Ongoing advisory or support | Define included hours and out-of-scope triggers |

---

## Commercial Guardrails

- **Minimum margin:** 40% — do not discount below this threshold without Practice Lead approval
- **Minimum engagement size:** 5 days
- **Travel and accommodation:** billed at cost; not subject to margin
- **Subcontractor costs:** marked up at standard margin

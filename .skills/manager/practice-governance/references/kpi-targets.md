# Practice KPI Definitions and Targets

Reference for the practice-governance skill. Defines each KPI, its calculation, target, and the action threshold that triggers a rating change.

---

## Delivery Quality KPIs

### On-Time Delivery Rate
**Definition:** Percentage of active projects that are tracking to deliver within the originally contracted timeline (or last approved timeline following an accepted change request).
**Calculation:** (Projects on track / Total active projects) × 100
**Target:** ≥85%
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: ≥85%
- Amber: 70–84%
- Red: <70%

### On-Budget Rate
**Definition:** Percentage of active projects that are tracking within the contracted budget (or last approved budget following an accepted change request).
**Calculation:** (Projects within budget / Total active projects) × 100
**Target:** ≥85%
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: ≥85%
- Amber: 70–84%
- Red: <70%

### Change Request Volume
**Definition:** Number of approved change requests issued in the period. High volume may indicate estimation or scoping issues.
**Target:** Baseline (track trend, not absolute number)
**Alert:** >3 CRs per project in a single engagement warrants a lessons-learned review.

### Escalation Rate
**Definition:** Number of customer escalations (formal complaints, executive escalations, or contract disputes) in the period.
**Target:** 0
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: 0
- Amber: 1 (resolved within 5 business days)
- Red: 1 unresolved, or 2+ in the period

## Financial Performance KPIs

### Revenue Recognised
**Definition:** Total revenue recognised (invoiced and earned) in the reporting period, net of GST.
**Target:** As per financial forecast
**Rating thresholds:** Based on variance to forecast (see Forecast Accuracy below)

### Delivery Margin
**Definition:** Revenue recognised minus direct delivery cost (labour at cost rate, subcontractors, direct expenses), expressed as a percentage of revenue.
**Calculation:** (Revenue − Direct costs) / Revenue × 100
**Target:** ≥40%
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: ≥40%
- Amber: 35–39%
- Red: <35%

### Forecast Accuracy
**Definition:** Variance between forecast revenue for the period and actual revenue recognised.
**Calculation:** (Actual − Forecast) / Forecast × 100
**Target:** Variance within ±10%
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: Within ±10%
- Amber: ±11–20%
- Red: >±20% (either direction)

### Deferred Revenue
**Definition:** Contracted revenue not yet recognised (work invoiced in advance or milestone payments received ahead of delivery).
**Target:** Track and report; no absolute target
**Alert:** Deferred revenue decreasing month-on-month without new contract wins = pipeline risk.

## Team Health KPIs

### Billable Utilisation
**Definition:** Percentage of available working hours billed to customers, averaged across the delivery team.
**Calculation:** (Total billable hours / Total available hours) × 100
**Target:** 70–80%
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: 70–80%
- Amber: 60–69% (under-utilised) or 81–89% (approaching overload)
- Red: <60% (significant under-utilisation) or ≥90% (burnout risk)

**Available hours:** Total working hours minus approved leave, public holidays, and agreed non-billable time (training, internal meetings, practice development).

### Over-Utilised Engineers
**Definition:** Number of team members with billable utilisation ≥90% for two or more consecutive weeks.
**Target:** 0
**Alert:** Any engineer at ≥90% for 2+ weeks — review workload allocation immediately.

### Under-Utilised Engineers
**Definition:** Number of team members with billable utilisation <50% for two or more consecutive weeks.
**Target:** 0
**Alert:** Investigate root cause — skills mismatch, pipeline gap, or onboarding issue.

### Team Attrition
**Definition:** Number of voluntary departures in the period.
**Target:** 0 voluntary departures per quarter
**Alert:** Any voluntary departure triggers an exit interview and retention risk review for remaining team.

## Customer Satisfaction KPIs

### Net Promoter Score (NPS)
**Definition:** Customer likelihood to recommend The Instillery, measured on a 0–10 scale. NPS = % Promoters (9–10) − % Detractors (0–6).
**Target:** ≥50
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: ≥50
- Amber: 30–49
- Red: <30

### At-Risk Accounts
**Definition:** Number of accounts rated Amber or Red in the most recent client-success review.
**Target:** 0 Red accounts; ≤10% of accounts Amber
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: 0 Red, ≤10% Amber
- Amber: 0 Red, >10% Amber
- Red: 1+ Red accounts

### Repeat Engagement Rate
**Definition:** Percentage of customers who have engaged The Instillery for two or more separate projects.
**Target:** ≥60% (establish baseline in first year)
**Alert:** Declining repeat rate indicates customer satisfaction or relationship issues.

## Pipeline KPIs

### Weighted Pipeline Value
**Definition:** Total value of active opportunities, weighted by close probability.
**Calculation:** Σ (Opportunity value × Close probability %)
**Target:** ≥3× the current quarter revenue target
**Rating thresholds:**
- Green: ≥3× quarterly target
- Amber: 2–2.9× quarterly target
- Red: <2× quarterly target

### Pipeline Coverage Ratio
**Definition:** Weighted pipeline as a multiple of the quarterly revenue target.
**Target:** ≥3×
**Alert:** Coverage below 2× requires immediate sales action — do not wait for next governance cycle.

### New Opportunities Added
**Definition:** Number of new qualified opportunities added to the pipeline in the period.
**Target:** Baseline (track trend; no absolute target until baseline is established)

### Partner-Registered Deals
**Definition:** Number of active opportunities with deal registration submitted to a partner (Microsoft, AWS, Google, AvePoint, Wiz, Zscaler).
**Target:** All eligible opportunities should be registered
**Alert:** Unregistered deals on partner technology are forfeiting incentives and co-sell support.

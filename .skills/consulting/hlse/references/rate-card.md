## Delivery Model Guidance

| Model | When to Use | Margin Protection |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| Time & Materials (T&M) | Unclear or evolving scope | Bill actuals; invoice monthly |
| Fixed Price | Well-defined, bounded scope | Add 15–20% contingency to T&M estimate; document all assumptions |
| Milestone-Based | Multi-phase delivery with clear gates | Define objective acceptance criteria per milestone |
| Retainer | Ongoing advisory or support | Define included hours and out-of-scope triggers |

---

## Commercial Guardrails

- **Minimum margin:** 40% — do not discount below this threshold without Practice Lead approval
- **Minimum engagement size:** 5 days — smaller engagements carry disproportionate overhead
- **Travel and accommodation:** billed at cost; not subject to margin calculation
- **Subcontractor costs:** marked up at standard margin; disclose to customer if requested
- **Contingency:** must be labelled explicitly in fixed-price proposals — never buried in rates

## All-of-Government (AoG) Panel Rates

AoG panel engagements are subject to the schedule of rates submitted and approved at panel registration. These rates may differ from standard commercial rates above. Always verify the current approved AoG rate schedule before quoting on a panel opportunity — do not use the commercial rate card for panel pricing without confirming the panel-approved rates first.

## HLSE Estimation Guide

Use ranges, not point estimates, for all HLSE-stage pricing:

| Phase | Typical Duration Range | Notes |
|-------|----------------------|-------|
| Discovery & Planning | 3–10 days | Scales with customer complexity and number of stakeholders |
| Design (HLD) | 3–8 days | Scales with solution breadth and integration points |
| Implementation | 5–40+ days | Highly variable — break down by workstream |
| Testing | 2–5 days | Minimum; add more for regulated or complex environments |
| Deployment | 1–5 days | Depends on cutover complexity and rollback requirements |
| Training & Handover | 1–3 days | Add more for large user bases or complex operational handover |

At HLSE stage, always present a low and high scenario. The range reflects scope uncertainty, not pricing flexibility.

# NZ and Australian Contract Law — Reference

Key legislation relevant to The Instillery's commercial agreements. Use during commercial review to identify compliance obligations and flag missing protections.

---

## New Zealand

### Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017 (CCLA)
**Relevance:** Governs formation, interpretation, performance, and remedies for all commercial contracts in NZ.
- Electronic contracts and e-signatures are valid under Part 4
- Implied terms may be read into contracts even if not stated
- Exclusion clauses must be clearly expressed to be enforceable

**Review check:** Ensure governing law clause specifies New Zealand law for NZ-based engagements.

---

### Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 (CGA)
**Relevance:** Applies to services supplied to consumers. Guarantees of acceptable quality and fitness for purpose.
- **Does NOT apply** when services are acquired for business purposes — confirm customer type before assessing CGA exposure
- Cannot be contracted out of where CGA applies
- Remedy for breach: right to have service re-performed or claim damages

**Review check:** If contracting with an individual or small business that may be a "consumer," assess whether CGA exclusion clauses are valid.

---

### Fair Trading Act 1986 (FTA)
**Relevance:** Prohibits misleading and deceptive conduct in trade. Applies to statements in proposals, marketing material, and contract representations.
- Covers representations about capabilities, timelines, pricing, and outcomes
- Applies regardless of intent — an innocent misrepresentation can still breach the FTA
- Remedies: injunction, compensation, or contract cancellation

**Review check:** Ensure scope descriptions, outcome claims, and capability representations in the agreement are accurate and supportable.

---

### Privacy Act 2020
**Relevance:** Governs collection, storage, use, and disclosure of personal information.
- 13 Information Privacy Principles (IPPs) govern data handling
- Mandatory breach notification to the Privacy Commissioner and affected individuals for notifiable privacy breaches
- Data processing agreements (DPAs) required when sharing personal data with third parties

**Review check:** If the engagement involves processing customer personal data, confirm a DPA or data handling clause is included. Check data residency obligations (NZ data must generally stay in NZ unless consent obtained).

---

### Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 (HSWA)
**Relevance:** Applies to on-site project delivery where The Instillery personnel work at a customer's premises.
- Both The Instillery and the customer are PCBUs (Persons Conducting a Business or Undertaking) with shared duties
- Site-specific induction and hazard identification obligations apply

**Review check:** For on-site delivery, confirm the contract or site agreement addresses HSWA obligations and indemnity for on-site incidents.

---

## Australia

### Australian Consumer Law (ACL) — Schedule 2 to the Competition and Consumer Act 2010
**Relevance:** National consumer protection law applying across all states and territories.
- Consumer guarantees for services: due care and skill, fitness for purpose, supply within reasonable time
- **Does NOT apply** to services acquired for business purposes (B2B commercial engagements generally excluded)
- Cannot be contracted out of in consumer contexts

**Review check:** Same as CGA above — confirm customer is a business, not a consumer.

---

### Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) + Privacy Amendment (Notifiable Data Breaches) Act 2017
**Relevance:** Governs personal information handling for Australian entities.
- 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) govern collection, use, and disclosure
- Notifiable Data Breach (NDB) scheme: must notify the OAIC and affected individuals of eligible breaches
- Cross-border disclosure obligations — personal data sent overseas must meet equivalent protections

**Review check:** For AU engagements involving personal data, confirm the contract includes NDB obligations and appropriate data handling clauses.

---

### Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth) + State Equivalents
**Relevance:** Electronic signatures and contracts are valid in most commercial contexts.
- Exceptions: deeds, wills, powers of attorney (may require wet signature depending on state)
- Confirm the contract execution method is valid for the document type

## Key Clauses and Standards

### Liability

| Clause | Recommended Position |
|--------|-------------------|
| Liability cap | Cap at total fees paid in the preceding 12 months; exclude consequential and indirect loss |
| PI insurance | Minimum $2M per claim — confirm with current policy |
| PL insurance | Minimum $2M per claim |
| Cyber insurance | Minimum $1M — increasing to $2M for data-heavy engagements |
| Indemnities | Mutual where possible; avoid unilateral and uncapped indemnities |

### Intellectual Property

| Scenario | Position |
|----------|---------|
| Work product created for the customer | IP vests in customer on full payment |
| The Instillery background IP, tools, and methodologies | Retained by The Instillery; licence granted for use |
| Third-party tools and software | Subject to third-party licence terms — do not assign |

### Payment and Termination

| Clause | Recommended Position |
|--------|-------------------|
| Payment terms | Net 20 days maximum |
| Late payment interest | Agreed rate or refer to NZ Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act |
| Termination for convenience | 30-day written notice minimum; payment for all work completed to date |
| Termination for cause | 14-day cure period for remediable breaches |
| Dispute resolution | Negotiation → mediation → arbitration; NZ or AU governing law and jurisdiction |

---

## Common Red Flags

- Uncapped liability with no exclusion for consequential loss
- IP assignment that includes The Instillery's background tools and methodologies
- Payment terms beyond 30 days net
- Unilateral termination rights with no payment for work in progress
- Indemnities that survive termination indefinitely without a time cap
- Governing law in a foreign jurisdiction (US, UK) for NZ/AU-based work

## Proposal Quality Checklist

Before sending:

- [ ] Executive summary written last and reads as a standalone summary
- [ ] Customer's situation described in their language — not ours
- [ ] Every phase has an outcome, not just activities
- [ ] Pricing is correctly calculated at ≥40% margin
- [ ] Assumptions are specific and documented
- [ ] Exclusions are explicit — not left to interpretation
- [ ] Proposal is addressed to a named individual
- [ ] No internal jargon, template placeholder text, or `[PLACEHOLDER]` remains
- [ ] Version and valid-until date are set
- [ ] Reviewed by at least one other person before sending

# Account Tier Definitions

Reference for the account-planning skill. Use these criteria to classify accounts and calibrate the planning depth and review cadence for each.

---

## Tier Classification

| Tier | Annual Revenue | Planning Depth | Review Cadence | Account Manager Time |
|------|---------------|---------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Strategic | >$500k NZD/yr | Full account plan | Monthly | High — dedicated focus |
| Growth | $100k–$500k/yr | Focused plan (whitespace + stakeholders) | Quarterly | Medium |
| Maintain | <$100k/yr | Health check only | Half-yearly | Low — reactive |

---

## Tier Criteria in Detail

### Strategic Tier

**Revenue threshold:** >$500,000 NZD per year (recognised, not contracted)

**Additional qualifying criteria (any 2 of the following):**
- Active across 2+ service pillars (Security, Optimisation, Governance)
- Consuming managed services in addition to project-based engagements
- Operating in both NZ and AU with The Instillery
- Strategic reference customer or logo account
- Significant upsell/cross-sell potential within 12 months

**What a Strategic account plan includes:**
- Full stakeholder map (all levels, all business units)
- Comprehensive whitespace analysis against full service portfolio
- Competitive landscape assessment
- 12-month engagement calendar
- Partner co-sell strategy
- Executive relationship programme
- Quarterly EBR (Executive Business Review)

---

### Growth Tier

**Revenue threshold:** $100,000–$500,000 NZD per year

**Qualifying criteria:**
- Active project engagement or recent project completion
- Demonstrated appetite for additional services
- Accessible stakeholders at decision-maker level

**What a Growth account plan includes:**
- Focused whitespace analysis (top 3 opportunities)
- Primary stakeholder map
- 6-month opportunity targets
- Quarterly account review (internal)
- Bi-annual customer check-in meeting

---

### Maintain Tier

**Revenue threshold:** <$100,000 NZD per year

**Qualifying criteria:**
- Single-project or transactional relationship
- No active expansion signals
- Low strategic importance

**What a Maintain account check includes:**
- Account health rating (client-success skill)
- Annual customer touchpoint
- Pipeline: opportunistic — respond to inbound requests
- Escalate to Growth tier if revenue grows or expansion signals emerge

---

## Tier Movement

Accounts should be reviewed for tier movement at the start of each planning cycle (typically annually) and when a significant event occurs:

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Revenue grows past tier threshold | Uplift to next tier in next planning cycle |
| Revenue drops below tier threshold for 2 consecutive years | Downgrade at next planning cycle |
| Strategic account lost major engagement or key stakeholder | Review for downgrade |
| Maintain account signals expansion intent | Uplift immediately; start Growth plan |
| Account at risk of churn | Escalate to client-success process regardless of tier |

---

## Revenue Calculation Notes

- **Annual revenue** = recognised revenue in the preceding 12 months (not contracted value, which may not yet be earned)
- **Include:** all PS engagements, managed services fees, and software resale margin
- **Exclude:** pass-through costs (customer-funded licences at zero margin, direct expenses)
- **Multi-entity:** if a customer group has multiple subsidiaries, sum the group revenue for tier classification; maintain separate account records per entity

# Insurance Minimums — Reference

Insurance requirements for government and enterprise engagements. Verify current policy coverage before submitting any tender response or signing a contract.

---

## The Instillery Standard Insurance Coverage

Confirm actual coverage limits with the Operations team before including in a proposal or tender response — limits below are indicative minimums and may have been updated.

| Policy | Coverage | Typical Minimum |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Professional Indemnity (PI) | Claims arising from professional advice, errors, or omissions | $2,000,000 per claim |
| Public Liability (PL) | Third-party bodily injury or property damage | $2,000,000 per claim |
| Cyber Liability | Data breach, cyber incident response, regulatory fines | $1,000,000 per claim |
| Employer's Liability | Employee injury claims | Covered by ACC in NZ |
| Workers' Compensation | As above (AU engagements) | State-specific |

## Government Contract Requirements

### All-of-Government Panels (NZ)

Minimum insurance requirements vary by panel. Common requirements:

| Panel | PI Minimum | PL Minimum | Cyber |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| ICT Professional Services (PS3) | $1,000,000 | $1,000,000 | Not always mandated |
| Cloud Services | $2,000,000 | $2,000,000 | Often required |
| Cyber Security Services | $2,000,000 | $2,000,000 | Required |

**Always verify** the specific panel's contract schedule — minimums are defined per panel.

### NZ Central Government (non-AoG)
Standard government contract templates (e.g. NZGPT) typically require:
- PI: ≥$1,000,000 per claim
- PL: ≥$1,000,000 per occurrence
- Motor vehicle: if vehicles used in delivery

### NZ Local Government
Requirements vary by council. Common minimums:
- PI: ≥$1,000,000
- PL: ≥$2,000,000 for any on-site delivery

### Australian Federal Government
- PI: AUD $2,000,000 per claim (minimum; some contracts require $5M+)
- PL: AUD $10,000,000 per occurrence (standard for Commonwealth contracts)
- Workers' Compensation: as required by state

### Australian State Government
Requirements vary by state. Queensland, NSW, and Victoria commonly require:
- PI: AUD $2,000,000
- PL: AUD $10,000,000

## Enterprise Customer Requirements

Large enterprise customers (banks, telcos, utilities, healthcare) may impose contractual insurance minimums that exceed government requirements. Common enterprise requirements:

| Customer Type | PI Typical | PL Typical | Cyber Typical |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| Financial services | $5,000,000 | $5,000,000 | $2,000,000 |
| Healthcare / DHB | $2,000,000 | $5,000,000 | $2,000,000 |
| Utilities / Infrastructure | $5,000,000 | $10,000,000 | $1,000,000 |
| Standard enterprise | $2,000,000 | $2,000,000 | $1,000,000 |

If a customer requires limits above The Instillery's current coverage, escalate to the Practice Lead before committing to the contract — increasing coverage limits requires insurer approval and will affect premium.

## Certificate of Currency

For most tender responses and enterprise contracts, a Certificate of Currency (CoC) is required as evidence of current insurance cover. The CoC is issued by the insurer and shows:
- Insured name
- Policy type and number
- Coverage limits
- Policy period (must be current at submission date)

**Process:** Request the CoC from the Operations team — allow 2–3 business days. Do not submit a CoC from a prior period.

## Common Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|-------|-----------|
| Required limit exceeds current coverage | Escalate to Practice Lead; get insurer quote for higher limit |
| CoC expired or covering wrong entity name | Request updated CoC from Operations |
| Customer requires to be named as additional insured | Discuss with insurer; additional insured endorsements are common but must be approved |
| Engagement involves US-based activity | US-specific coverage may be required — PI policies typically exclude US jurisdiction by default |

# All-of-Government (AoG) Panels — Reference

New Zealand government procurement reference for RFP responses involving AoG panel opportunities.

---

## What is the All-of-Government Programme?

The All-of-Government (AoG) programme is administered by New Zealand Government Procurement (NZGP), part of the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE). AoG contracts establish pre-negotiated terms, pricing, and supplier lists that government agencies can use without running a separate full procurement process.

Benefits for suppliers on the panel:
- Streamlined customer procurement — agencies can engage directly without a new tender
- Pre-negotiated contract terms reduce legal overhead
- Panel status serves as a credential in private sector proposals

Benefits for agencies:
- Reduced procurement cost and time
- Pre-vetted suppliers with agreed terms
- Compliance with the Government Rules of Sourcing

## Relevant AoG Panels for The Instillery

The following panels are most relevant to The Instillery's services. Verify current registration status and panel schedules before quoting.

### ICT Professional Services (PS3)
**Category:** IT consulting, advisory, and professional services
**Typical scope:** Strategy, architecture, project management, business analysis, change management, training
**Relevance:** High — most consulting and engineering engagements fall under this panel
**Procurement path:** Agencies approach registered suppliers directly or run a panel-based mini-competition

### Cloud Services Panel
**Category:** Public cloud infrastructure, platform, and SaaS services
**Typical scope:** Azure, AWS, Google Cloud services; cloud migration; managed cloud
**Relevance:** High — Azure CSP status supports eligibility; cloud engagements may use this panel
**Procurement path:** Direct call-off or mini-competition for larger engagements

### Cyber Security Services
**Category:** Security consulting, assessment, managed security services, incident response
**Typical scope:** Security assessments, penetration testing, SIEM/SOC services, security architecture
**Relevance:** High — aligns to Security pillar; Wiz and Zscaler engagements may qualify
**Procurement path:** Panel-based mini-competition for most engagements

### Telecommunications-as-a-Service (TaaS)
**Category:** Connectivity, telephony, and communications services
**Relevance:** Medium — Microsoft Teams Calling / Teams Phone engagements may be in scope

## AoG Procurement Rules for Suppliers

### Schedule of Rates
AoG contracts specify approved rates that differ from commercial rate cards. Always check the current approved schedule before quoting on a panel opportunity. Submitting commercial rates on a panel quote is a compliance breach.

### Mini-Competition Process
For engagements above a panel-specific threshold, agencies run a mini-competition among panel suppliers:
1. Agency issues a mini-competition brief to all (or selected) panel suppliers
2. Suppliers respond within the specified timeframe (typically 5–15 business days)
3. Agency evaluates and selects based on criteria in the mini-competition brief
4. Winning supplier receives a call-off purchase order under the panel agreement

### Direct Engagement
Below the mini-competition threshold, agencies may engage a panel supplier directly. The supplier must still comply with panel terms and rates.

### Mandatory Requirements for Panel Responses
- Company registered in New Zealand (or meets AoG eligibility criteria)
- Insurance: PI ≥ $1M, PL ≥ $1M (check panel-specific minimums — see insurance-minimums.md)
- No conflicts of interest that would prevent impartial delivery
- Personnel named in responses must be available for the engagement duration

## Writing for Government Evaluators

Government RFP evaluations are structured and scored. Apply these principles:

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|------------|
| Answer the question | Map every response paragraph to a specific criterion — evaluators score against criteria |
| Evidence over assertion | Reference specific past projects, certifications, or methodologies |
| Plain English | Avoid jargon — evaluation panels include non-technical members |
| Compliance first | Address mandatory criteria completely before addressing desirables |
| No assumptions | If something is unclear, seek clarification via the question process |

## AoG Response Checklist

Before submitting any AoG or government response:

- [ ] Verified current panel registration is active
- [ ] Confirmed applicable panel and call-off type (direct or mini-competition)
- [ ] Used AoG-approved schedule of rates (not commercial rate card)
- [ ] Insurance certificates are current and meet panel minimums
- [ ] All mandatory criteria addressed with pass evidence
- [ ] Response submitted via GETS or specified portal before deadline
- [ ] Authorised company representative has reviewed and approved submission
